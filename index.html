The tile, alive.

A cross-stitch tile — every line a row of tiny x stitches — drawn as dither dots. Starbursts, diamonds and medallions stay anchored by their centres while their outer arms reach, contracting and pulling, dragging each body up, down, sideways and every angle between, like micro-organisms. Move your pointer to spook them. Click to poke.

— fps
